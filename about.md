Linux Notes is a free online publication dedicated to Linux, open source
software, and command-line tools. It publishes in-depth tutorials,
practical how-to guides, software reviews, and articles covering Linux
distributions, system administration, command-line tools, and open source
applications.

We believe open source software gives everyone the freedom to learn,
understand, and control the technology they use. That's why Linux Notes
focuses on sharing practical knowledge that helps readers build confidence
with Linux and the wider open source ecosystem.

Whether you're just getting started with Linux or have years of experience,
the goal is to provide clear, accurate, and practical content that helps
you solve real problems and better understand how Linux systems work.

Linux Notes aims to build a long-lasting knowledge base of evergreen
articles that remain useful for years. Every tutorial is written with
an emphasis on clarity, technical accuracy, and real-world examples.

## About the Founder

I started Linux Notes with one simple goal: to publish clear, practical,
and trustworthy Linux tutorials that help people solve real problems
and better understand their systems.

Over the years I've spent countless hours learning Linux, experimenting
with different distributions, configuring systems, and exploring open
source software. This website is a place to document that experience and
share it with others in the hope that it saves readers time and helps
them get more out of Linux.

Whether you're looking for a command reference, a troubleshooting guide,
or a step-by-step tutorial, I hope Linux Notes helps you learn something
new and solve problems more efficiently.
